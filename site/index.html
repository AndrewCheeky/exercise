How the index works

7–10 Go · 5–6 Caution · 0–4 Avoid. Each window starts at 10 and loses points for heat, humidity, rain, thunderstorms, heat stress (WBGT), sticky dew points, strong sun, gusts, recent or heavy rain and poor air quality. Thunderstorms or dangerous heat cap it at 4. Riding uses the same rules, plus small changes for air density and rain just after the window.
