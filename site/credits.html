Credits

Caribbean Islands We Outside is built on openly available data and software. Thank you to the people and organisations behind them.

Weather and sea forecasts

Weather, marine and air quality forecasts: Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0), which combines models from national weather services.

Air quality and Saharan dust forecasts come from the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS), through Open-Meteo.

Live lightning

Lightning data: Xweather. Live lightning currently covers Jamaica only.

Scoring

The 0–10 score grew out of the Go Run Index in our daily runner and cyclist email. It uses standard formulas for heat index, dew point and an estimate of wet-bulb globe temperature (WBGT), tuned for Caribbean heat and humidity.

Software and fonts

  • Hosting: GitHub Pages; forecasts are rebuilt by GitHub Actions and Google Apps Script.
  • Typefaces: Oswald by Vernon Adams and contributors (SIL Open Font License 1.1), and Roboto by Christian Robertson (Apache License 2.0), served by Google Fonts.
